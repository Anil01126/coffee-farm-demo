Section 02
How Offline-First Works
In rural Ethiopia, internet is unreliable. The system is designed so that field workers never need connectivity to do their jobs. Data syncs automatically when a connection is found — even days later.
Why this matters for your farm
Supervisors carry tablets into the field at dawn. They log attendance, cherry weights, and plot activities all day — with no signal. When they return to the processing station (where Wi-Fi exists), everything uploads automatically in the background. The manager sees it on the web dashboard in real time.
1
Work Offline
Supervisor logs data on tablet. Stored locally on device — no internet needed.
2
Queue Builds
All entries are added to a sync queue. Nothing is lost even if the tablet runs out of battery.
3
Signal Found
When Wi-Fi or 4G is detected, sync starts automatically in the background.
4
Manager Sees It
Dashboard updates instantly. Reports, payroll, and supply chain all reflect latest field data.
1
Tablet per 10–15 workers
Supervisors manage attendance and harvest for their section. Workers don't need personal devices — just one shared tablet per group.
0
Data lost on connectivity drop
The sync queue persists even if the tablet is powered off. Everything uploads the next time it connects — days later if needed.
~5s
Average sync time per day's data
A full day of logs for one section syncs in under 5 seconds. Minimal data usage — optimised for slow 3G connections.
Section 03
Three Core Modules
The system covers the full lifecycle of the farm — from hiring a seasonal worker to exporting a traced lot of green beans.
👷
Digital Labor Management
End-to-end worker lifecycle from recruitment through daily operations to payroll disbursement.
  • Worker profiles with photo & ID
  • Seasonal recruitment pipeline
  • Daily attendance (offline, tablet)
  • Cherry picking piece-rate tracking
  • Payroll with M-Pesa disbursement
  • Leave & absence management
  • Section & supervisor assignment
🌿
Farm Operations Digitization
Complete visibility over what is happening on every plot, every day — from GPS mapping to task completion.
  • GPS-mapped farm plot boundaries
  • Per-plot harvest weight logging
  • Fertilizer & input usage tracking
  • Pest & disease incident reporting
  • Task assignment & completion
  • Irrigation & pruning schedules
  • Historical yield per plot per season
🔗
Supply Chain Traceability
Track every lot of coffee from the tree it was picked from to the buyer who receives it — fully documented.
  • Lot creation at cherry harvest
  • Wet mill intake & fermentation log
  • Dry mill moisture & drying days
  • Grading & cupping score records
  • QR-coded export bags
  • ICO & export documentation
  • Buyer portal with lot trace link

Phase 1 — MVP Weeks 1–10
Core Labor & Harvest
Worker registry & profiles Offline attendance app Harvest weight logging Basic payroll export Farm plot map Web manager dashboard
Phase 2 Weeks 11–18

Phase 3 Weeks 19–26

Full lot traceability QR bag labelling Export documentation Buyer portal (public trace) Advanced analytics
Recommended Start: Paid Pilot
Before full rollout, we recommend a 6-week paid pilot with one section of the farm (20–30 workers). This proves the system works in your specific environment before committing to the full deployment.
✓ Real workers, real data — not a lab test
✓ Identifies any local workflow adjustments needed
✓ Builds team familiarity before full scale
✓ Low risk — you see results before full investment
Hardware Needed
• 6–8 Android tablets (10", budget models ~$80–120 each)
• Basic Wi-Fi router at processing station
• Portable chargers for field use
• No servers on-site — everything is in the cloud
